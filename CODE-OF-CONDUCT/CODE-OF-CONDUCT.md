# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct defines the standards of behavior expected from all
participants who use, contribute to, review, distribute, or otherwise interact
with this project. The goal is to maintain a professional, respectful,
collaborative, and legally compliant environment.

This project and all related materials are the intellectual property of
Dr. Jeffrey Chijioke-Uche, unless expressly stated otherwise in writing.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces and interactions, including:

- Source code repositories and branches
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Documentation and release artifacts
- Chat, email, meetings, and collaboration channels
- Any public or private communication representing this project

## 3. Our Standards

### 3.1 Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Act professionally and respectfully in all communications
- Assume positive intent while discussing technical decisions
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith
- Use clear, accurate, and responsible language
- Respect legal, security, and compliance requirements
- Protect confidential and proprietary information

### 3.2 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not allowed:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks, insults, or abusive language
- Deliberate misrepresentation of work, authorship, or credentials
- Sharing confidential, sensitive, or proprietary information without approval
- Unauthorized use, copying, modification, or distribution of project assets
- Any illegal activity or behavior that harms the project or its participants

## 4. Intellectual Property and Attribution

### 4.1 Ownership

All source code, scripts, documentation, designs, and related materials in this
project are proprietary and constitute intellectual property owned by
Dr. Jeffrey Chijioke-Uche, unless otherwise identified in writing.

### 4.2 Respect for Intellectual Property

Participants must:

- Preserve all copyright, attribution, and proprietary notices
- Use project materials only as authorized by project licensing terms
- Obtain written permission before redistributing or repurposing proprietary
	materials beyond approved use
- Avoid submitting third-party content unless they have legal rights and
	required permissions to do so

### 4.3 No Unauthorized Derivative Use

No participant may create, publish, sell, sublicense, or distribute derivative
works based on this project's proprietary content without prior written
authorization from the intellectual property owner.

## 5. Security and Responsible Use

Participants must:

- Follow secure development and operational practices
- Promptly report known security vulnerabilities through approved channels
- Avoid introducing malicious code, backdoors, or unsafe dependencies
- Handle credentials, tokens, and secrets in a secure manner

## 6. Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this Code, report it as soon
as possible to the project owner:

- Contact: Dr. Jeffrey Chijioke-Uche
- Channel: Approved project communication channel or designated email

Include relevant facts, timestamps, and supporting context when possible.
Reports will be handled promptly and as confidentially as practical.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations may result in corrective actions, including:

- Private warning and required corrective behavior
- Temporary suspension of collaboration or repository access
- Permanent removal from project participation
- Legal action where intellectual property, confidentiality, or law is violated

Enforcement decisions are made by the project owner and are based on severity,
frequency, impact, and good-faith cooperation.

## 8. Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against any person who reports a concern or participates in an
investigation in good faith is prohibited.

## 9. Acknowledgment

By participating in this project, you agree to comply with this Code of
Conduct, respect the rights and dignity of others, and honor the intellectual
property rights of Dr. Jeffrey Chijioke-Uche.
